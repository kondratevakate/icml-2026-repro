M<sub>t</sub>) regret analysis of Algorithm 1 for the BOT problem,
which shows that EntUCB matches the bounds in classical linear bandits despite the infinite-dimensional and non-
Hilbertian nature of the problem. We also show (deferred

## 4.1. Regret for Bandit Optimal Transport

Let us turn first to the BOT problem and Algorithm 1. In
Section 3.2, we showed that the confidence sets were valid
in the frequency domain (see Lemma C.2). However, ensuring a confidence set is valid is not sufficient to ensure
low regret, we also have to ensure that the size of this confidence set is small enough to control the error of the agent.
In the frequency domain, we can follow the standard arguments of Abbasi-Yadkori (2012) to bound the size of the
confidence set in terms of the width β<sub>t</sub>(δ), see Lemma D.1.

If η is chosen in (1/2, 1), Theorem 4.1 the trajectorial
term is dominant and the bound matches (Abbasi-Yadkori,
2012), showing that the BOT problem follows the same
fundamental structure as the classical Hilbert space linear
bandits. Notice that this result required significantly more
work than Hilbert space linear bandits, due to the need to
find an approximation regime in which a Hilbertian structure is valid. That this approximation can be made a lower
order term in the regret is a consequence of inherent regularity of the BOT problem, as shown by Lemma C.3.
