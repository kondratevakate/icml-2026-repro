# Study registry

One row per frozen protocol. "Frozen" is the commit that froze the protocol text; amendments and notes are
dated inside the protocol file. "Rerun" is the step name in `reproduce.py`, which reruns the analysis from
the committed coding sheets and downloaded inputs and compares the outputs with the committed results.
Coding by language-model subagents (blind coders) is the one step that is not rerun: its prompts and codes are
committed, and the analyses start from those codes.
