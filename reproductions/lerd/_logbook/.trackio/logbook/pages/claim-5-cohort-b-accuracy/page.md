**Paper claim (Table 2).** On AD Cohort B (Sadegh-Zadeh et al., 2023; 168
participants: 59 AD, 7 MCI, 102 HC), LERD attains 89.82 +/- 8.39% accuracy and
64.87 +/- 10.67% F1.

**Verdict: not evaluable (data not public).**

Cohort B is not the OpenNeuro dataset; it is the dataset from
[Sadegh-Zadeh et al., Diagnostics 13(3):477 (2023)](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4418/13/3/477)
(PubMed 36766582). Its Data Availability Statement reads verbatim
**"Not applicable"** - the dataset is not released. The underlying EEG traces to
that paper's reference [18] (Tzimourta et al., 2019), a private University of
Ioannina hospital dataset. No public mirror was located.

Because the raw EEG is unavailable, Claim 5 cannot be independently reproduced or
refuted and is scoped out of this reproduction.
