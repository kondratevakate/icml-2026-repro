**Anchored claim (verbatim).** "PyHealth 2.0 has an active open-source community
of 400+ members and provides 50+ tutorials alongside multi-language support via
an R interface (RHealth) (Section 5, Discussion)."

**Verdict -- INCONCLUSIVE.**

Two components have independent public evidence:

- the pinned release contains 184 `.py` or `.ipynb` files under `examples/`,
  clearing the paper's broader "50+ examples and tutorials" formulation;
- the paper bibliography links `v1xerunt/RHealth`, a public R package
  repository with dataset, task, model, trainer, and medical-code modules.

The remaining "400+ members" component is asserted in the paper abstract, but
the source provides no immutable roster, platform definition, snapshot, or
measurement procedure. Current stars, contributors, or Discord membership
would not reconstruct the historical paper-time quantity. Because the
challenge claim is conjunctive, the two auditable components are insufficient
for a full verification.
