Reproducing it needs those datasets, the full SEI method, and the competing baselines with matched training protocols — a GPU-scale effort. Here we validated the *mechanism* the claim rests on (Claims 1-2) on a CIFAR-100N toy; the medical-benchmark comparison is deferred and flagged as a blocker, not toy-substituted.
